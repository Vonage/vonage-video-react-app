# Time Left and the End of a Meeting

> To return to the main README, see [README](../README.md).

## The meeting timer

In a scheduled meeting, the toolbar shows how much time is left, next to the leave button. On a phone it shows in the
header at the top of the screen instead. Everyone in the meeting sees it. The time follows the server's clock, so a wrong clock on your device does not change it.

- More than an hour left: hours and minutes, for example **1 h 20 min left**.
- Less than an hour left: minutes, for example **40 min left**.
- The last ten minutes: minutes and seconds, for example **4:52 left**.

Meetings without a schedule have no end, so they show no timer.

## The end-of-meeting warning

In the last five minutes the timer turns amber, and a message tells everyone that the meeting is about to finish. The
message shows once. Nobody can join the meeting after its scheduled end.
