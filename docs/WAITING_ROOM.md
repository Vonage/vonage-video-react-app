# Waiting for a Scheduled Meeting

> To return to the main README, see [README](../README.md).

## Join automatically

If you open the link of a scheduled meeting before it starts, the Join button reads **Join at start**. It is enabled
once you have entered a valid name. Press it and the button switches to **Waiting to start…**, with a line under it
saying when you will join. When the meeting starts by the server's clock, you join without pressing anything else, so
you can wait in another tab.

- Press **Waiting to start…** again, or clear your name, before the start to cancel.
- If you did not press **Join at start**, the button reads **Join meeting** once the meeting has started.
- Meetings without a schedule, and meetings that have already started, show the usual **Join meeting** button.
