# Waiting for a Scheduled Meeting

> To return to the main README, see [README](../README.md).

## Join automatically

If you open the link of a scheduled meeting before it starts, the waiting room shows a **Join automatically when it
starts** checkbox under the Join button. It is off unless you tick it, and you can only tick it once you have entered
your name. When the meeting starts by the server's clock, you join without pressing Join, so you can wait in another
tab.

- Untick the box, or clear your name, before the start to cancel.
- The checkbox does not show for meetings without a schedule, or once a meeting has started or ended.
