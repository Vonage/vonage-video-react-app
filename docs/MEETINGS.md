# Creating and Sharing a Meeting

> To return to the main README, see [README](../README.md).

## Create a meeting

On the landing page, select **Create a new room**. The **Create a meeting** dialog opens on its **Details** step:

- **Meeting name** is optional. It becomes the room name, so it accepts lowercase letters, numbers, `-`, `_` and `+`.
  Leave it empty to get a random name.
- **Set a date and time** is off by default. Without a schedule the link never expires, and anyone with it can join at
  any time.

Select **Create meeting** to create it and move to the **Share** step.

## Schedule a meeting

Turn on **Set a date and time**, then pick the date, the start time and a duration of **1 hour**, **2 hours** or
**24 hours**. 1 hour is selected by default, and the start time cannot be in the past.

A scheduled link works only inside its window:

- Before the start, the meeting cannot be joined yet.
- From the start until the end, people join as with any other link.
- After the end, the meeting can no longer be joined.

The server enforces the schedule from the signed link, whether sign-in is on or off.

## Share the link

The dialog shows the meeting link, and for a scheduled meeting its start and duration. Select the copy button next to
the link to copy it to the clipboard, then send it to the people you want to invite.

- Everyone who opens the link joins as a moderator.
- An unscheduled link does not expire.
- The link is not saved on your device. Copy it before you close the dialog.

## Join or close

- **Join now** opens the meeting's waiting room.
- **Done** closes the dialog and keeps you on the landing page. You can still join later by opening the link you copied.
